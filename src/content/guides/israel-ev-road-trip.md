Israel's road network is compact and the EV infrastructure has grown fast: 163,000 electric vehicles were on Israeli roads by end of 2024 — a 70% jump year-on-year — and a new wave of fast chargers is rolling out through 2026. For tourists, this means renting an EV is now practical for most Israeli road-trip routes, and notably cheaper to run than a petrol equivalent on longer journeys.

## Is an EV right for your Israel trip?

An electric car works well for most Israel itineraries. The country is small: the main tourist triangle (Tel Aviv–Jerusalem–Haifa) spans roughly 150 km from corner to corner, and even the Galilee and Dead Sea routes are 150–180 km from Tel Aviv. Any full-size modern EV handles these comfortably without stopping to charge.

The one route that needs planning is the **southern Negev run to Eilat** (Highway 40 south of Mitzpe Ramon — see below). For everything else, an EV is straightforward.

[Not sure if you need a car at all?](/car-rental-israel) The cities are well-served by train; a car — EV or otherwise — is most valuable for the Galilee, Golan Heights, and Negev.

Filter by "electric" or "plug-in hybrid" at the vehicle-type step. PHEVs are a sensible hedge for Negev-heavy itineraries.

**Practical tips:**
- Book EVs 5–7 days ahead in peak season — stock is smaller than conventional fleets.
- Confirm the rental includes a Type 2 (Mennekes) charging cable in the car. Most do; a few economy operators don't.
- Note how the rental company handles Highway 6 tolls — the same transponder system applies to EVs. Ask upfront.

## Israel's EV charging networks

Three networks cover the country:

| Network | Type | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Doralon** | AC Level 2 (22 kW max) | Overnight / hotel parking; city parking garages |
| **Enerjoy** | DC fast (up to 150 kW) | Highway service stations; shopping centres |
| **BYD fast chargers** | DC fast (up to 120 kW) | Expanding at dealerships + service plazas |

## Best EV-friendly road trip routes

### Tel Aviv → Dead Sea / Masada (175 km each way)

The classic day-trip is easy on a single charge. Park at the Ein Bokek hotels strip on the Dead Sea; most have Level 2 chargers that add 40–60 km of range per hour. A two-hour lunch break at the Dead Sea beach recharges enough for the return. The [Negev distance calculator](/israel-distance-calculator) confirms the round trip is ~350 km — within range of nearly any modern EV.

### Tel Aviv → Galilee loop (Highway 6 north, 155 km to Tiberias)

Highway 6 (the north–south toll road) has Enerjoy fast chargers at the Hadera and Yokneam service plazas. From Tel Aviv you can reach [Tiberias](/tiberias-guide) or [Nazareth](/nazareth-travel-guide) without a charging stop; a 20-minute fast-charge at Yokneam is enough to extend into the Golan Heights the same day. [Galilee wine trail](/galilee-wine-trail) wineries increasingly have guest chargers — confirm when booking tastings.

### Haifa → Golan Heights loop (~200 km circuit)

Haifa's Hadar neighbourhood has multiple Doralon points; the Golan Heights has Enerjoy fast chargers at the Kazrin junction and at Neve Ativ (near Mount Hermon). A full Golan loop via [Golan hiking trails](/golan-heights-hiking-trails) is comfortably within range.

### Negev circuit — plan charging stops

The Negev is where range planning matters. The **northern Negev** (Tel Aviv → Beer Sheva → [Mitzpe Ramon](/mitzpe-ramon-guide)) is fine: 215 km from Tel Aviv, with chargers in Beer Sheva and at the Mitzpe Ramon visitors centre.

The **southern Negev run** (Mitzpe Ramon → Eilat, 240 km on [Highway 40](/negev-highway-40-guide)) has gaps south of Mitzpe Ramon. Options:
- Charge fully at the Mitzpe Ramon visitors centre charger before heading south.
- Use a plug-in hybrid to eliminate range anxiety on this leg.
- Break the journey at the Shaharut junction charger (roughly halfway).

For the full Negev south, the [3 days in the Negev](/3-days-in-negev) itinerary includes a charging logistics note per day.

## Range anxiety tips

- **Charge to 80–90% the night before** any long-distance day — regenerative braking makes up some of the difference on the hills between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem.
- **Air conditioning uses 10–15% extra range** in summer. The Dead Sea summer heat (38–44°C) is real: budget for it.
- **Parking garages in Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, and Haifa** (IEA-standard Level 2 posts) let you top up while sightseeing. Most charge per kWh at rates around ₪0.80–1.20/kWh — roughly one-third the petrol equivalent per km.

## Overnight charging at hotels

Dead Sea, Galilee lakefront, and Golan Heights hotels at four-star and above generally offer 1–4 Type 2 charging points at the car park — sometimes free for guests, sometimes at cost (₪0.80–1.20/kWh). Budget properties rarely have chargers; use EVChargers-IL to identify the nearest public charger before checking in.
